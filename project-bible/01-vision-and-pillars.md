# Vision and Pillars

The Last Phantasm is an original cinematic tactical RPG rooted in political dark fantasy. It prioritizes story-driven handcrafted missions, relationships, and choices with consequences.

## Product pillars

1. **Readable tactics.** Battlefield state and player intent remain legible; effects never obscure decisions.
2. **Authored momentum.** Handcrafted missions carry the campaign forward without required grinding.
3. **Character consequence.** Relationships and meaningful choices matter to the experience.
4. **Accessible performance.** The browser game remains responsive on Chromebook-class hardware.
